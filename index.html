Technical Specifications

Size Charts, Measurement Guides & Fit Science

Built for regular-proportioned medium & large dogs 7.5–50 kg (16–110 lbs). Not graded for chondrodystrophic/specialty builds (French Bulldogs, Corgis, Sighthounds) or giant breeds over 50 kg.

Measuring for Sleeves

  1. B · Limb Circumference (Primary) — Around the widest upper foreleg just below the elbow.
  2. A · Sleeve Length — Top of shoulder blade down along the front leg to desired cuff point.
  3. C · Cuff Opening — Ribbed cuff stretches comfortably; match or slightly exceed wrist girth.

Measuring for Shirts, Hoodies & 4-Leg

  1. G · Chest Girth (Primary) — Around the deepest part of the ribcage right behind the front legs.
  2. N · Neck Girth — Base of the neck where a collar naturally rests.
  3. Back Length — Base of neck to mid-ribcage (Half-Body Shirts) or base of tail (Hoodies, Vests & 4-Leg).
Fit Science — Why Retailers Should Guide Customers NOT to Oversize: A dog’s elbow joint sits high up near the chest line (not mid-leg like a human elbow). Choosing an oversized garment causes the collar and chest panel to sag downward, dropping the internal 3D pad below the elbow joint and causing paws to step into cuffs. Always recommend a proper chest fit with slight breathing room — neither baggy nor tight.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

For stocked designs, our wholesale MOQ is 6 pieces per style (sizes/colorways can be mixed) or 10 pieces mixed across the catalog. For custom prints or made-to-order restock runs, MOQ is 60 pieces per design (splittable across up to 2 colorways).

Can we order samples first?

Yes — standard paid sample orders are welcome so your team can evaluate fabric handfeel, 3-layer pad rebound, and breed fit firsthand. Domestic delivery to your designated freight forwarder warehouse in China is included free of charge.

Do you offer custom prints or private-label woven labels?

Yes. As an original design studio with dual production lines, we engineer custom prints on any of our proven garment blocks (60 pcs/design MOQ, ~4–5 weeks) and sew in buyer-supplied brand & care labels. Buyer acts as importer of record; we supply signed fiber-content declarations and care guidance.

Which dog breeds and weights do your patterns fit?

Our size systems cover regular-proportioned medium and large dogs from 7.5 kg to 50 kg (16–110 lbs) — including Border Collies, Spaniels, Australian Shepherds, Golden Retrievers, Labradors, Huskies, Standard Poodles, German Shepherds, Samoyeds, Malamutes, and Bernese Mountain Dogs. Patterns are not graded for specialty proportions (French Bulldogs, Corgis, Sighthounds) or giant breeds over 50 kg.

Can we use your photos and videos on our Shopify, Amazon, or boutique social channels?

Yes — wholesale partners receive full usage rights to our watermark-free white-background photos, worn lookbooks, and clean studio/field product videos ready for direct storefront embedding or local-language localization.
